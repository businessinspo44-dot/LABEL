Glossary

Plain-language definitions for common probability and gaming-math terms.

Term Meaning
Probability A measure of how likely an event is, from 0% to 100%.
RTP Theoretical long-run return expressed as a percentage of total stakes.
House edge The mathematical advantage under the stated rules and assumptions.
Expected value The probability-weighted average result across repeated trials.
Variance A measure of how widely outcomes are spread around their average.
Volatility An informal description of the size and frequency of result swings.
Turnover The cumulative amount wagered, including repeated use of returned funds.
Independent event An event whose probability is not changed by previous independent results.
Keep the scope in mind: mathematical averages describe repeated trials. They cannot tell you what the next random outcome will be.
