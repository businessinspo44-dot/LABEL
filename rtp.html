Return to Player (RTP)

RTP is a long-run theoretical percentage used to describe how much of total stakes a game is designed to return across a very large number of plays.

What a 96% RTP means

A theoretical RTP of 96% means the game model returns 96 units for every 100 units staked when measured over the very large sample assumed by its mathematics. It does not mean a person who stakes 100 will receive 96 back.

Important: RTP describes a long-run model. Individual results can be far above or below the theoretical percentage.

RTP and sample size

Small samples can differ sharply from the theoretical rate. As the number of independent trials becomes very large, observed averages may move closer to the mathematical expectation, but there is no fixed point at which a player's previous losses must be recovered.

Different rules, different RTP

Some games can have different theoretical returns depending on rules, pay tables or decisions. A published percentage should therefore be read together with the exact version and assumptions used to calculate it.

RTP is not a forecast

It cannot tell you the outcome of the next play, how long a session will last, or whether a specific person will finish ahead or behind.

Keep the scope in mind: mathematical averages describe repeated trials. They cannot tell you what the next random outcome will be.
