Variance & Volatility

Two games can have similar theoretical returns while producing very different patterns of short-term results.

What variance describes

Variance is a statistical measure of how spread out results are around their average. In plain terms, higher variance usually means outcomes can swing more widely.

RTP does not describe the ride

RTP concerns the long-run average. Variance concerns dispersion. A high-volatility model may have less frequent but larger outcomes, while a lower-volatility model may cluster outcomes more closely. These descriptions do not change the expected value by themselves.

Why this distinction matters

Looking only at RTP leaves out information about how unevenly results may arrive. Neither RTP nor variance can predict a particular session.

Keep the scope in mind: mathematical averages describe repeated trials. They cannot tell you what the next random outcome will be.
