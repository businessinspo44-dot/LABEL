About this site

A plain-language reference for gaming math

Plain Odds Guide explains probability, RTP, house edge and variance without promotions, operator rankings or claims about winning.

Editorial approach

Examples are intentionally simplified so the underlying calculation is easier to see. Real games can have additional rules, pay tables and assumptions. Where exact figures matter, readers should check the official rules and published mathematical information for that specific game.

What this site does not do

It does not provide betting systems, guaranteed-win methods, operator recommendations or predictions. Random outcomes remain uncertain.
